1. Introduction

Vyapar Vaani ("we", "our", "platform") is a voice-first WhatsApp commerce platform that enables rural Indian merchants to list and sell products online. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our services via WhatsApp Business API and our web marketplace.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following information when you interact with our platform:

  • Phone Number — Your WhatsApp phone number, used as your primary identifier
  • Voice Messages — Audio recordings you send via WhatsApp for product listing (transcribed and then deleted within 24 hours)
  • Product Information — Product names, prices, quantities, descriptions, and images you provide
  • PAN Card (for KYC) — PAN card photo for identity verification of sellers, processed via Amazon Textract OCR; original images stored securely in encrypted S3 buckets
  • Business Information — Seller name, business name, UPI ID (if provided for payments)
  • Order Data — Order details, delivery addresses, payment information for buyers
  • Conversation History — Chat interactions for contextual AI responses (retained for 30 days)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To verify seller identity via KYC (PAN card verification)
  • To transcribe voice messages and extract product information using AI
  • To create and manage your product catalog on the marketplace
  • To process and manage buyer orders
  • To send order notifications via WhatsApp
  • To provide personalized voice responses in your preferred language
  • To improve platform experience through conversation context

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure in the us-east-1 (N. Virginia) region:

  • Amazon DynamoDB — User profiles, product catalogs, orders, conversation history
  • Amazon S3 — Product images, KYC documents (encrypted at rest using AWS KMS)
  • AWS KMS — All sensitive data encrypted with customer-managed encryption keys

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+), IAM-based access controls, and regular security audits.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • WhatsApp Business API (Meta) — For messaging between sellers and the platform
  • Amazon Bedrock AI — For voice transcription, product description generation, and image enhancement
  • Amazon Textract — For PAN card OCR during KYC
  • Amazon Rekognition — For image quality analysis

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

6. WhatsApp Business API Usage

Our platform communicates with you through the WhatsApp Business API operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages sent through WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We access only the data you explicitly send to our business number.

7. Data Retention

  • Voice recordings — Deleted within 24 hours after transcription
  • Conversation history — Retained for 30 days
  • Product data — Retained as long as your account is active
  • KYC documents — Retained as required by Indian regulations
  • Order history — Retained for 1 year for dispute resolution

8. Your Rights

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (see Data Deletion)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
